About

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at Clemson University, under the supervision of Dr. Bart Knijnenburg. I work in the Humans and Technology Laboratory (HATLab) in the School of Computing. On the side, I happily wear a second hat as the lab's social media coordinator, where I get to celebrate our team's work with the world.

My research explores how people make privacy decisions and how technology can support them. I design personalized privacy education, study how visualization shapes user experience in recommender systems, and examine responsible practices for human-centered AI research.

Outside of research, I enjoy traveling and exploring new cultures, cooking for family gatherings, hiking, and reading about psychology and personal development.
